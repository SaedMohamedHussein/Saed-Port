Research

Graduate research in hybrid quantum-classical estimation and intelligent filtering.

Adaptive Variational Quantum Regression–Aided Extended Kalman Filter

A hybrid quantum-classical framework designed to improve nonlinear state estimation under time-varying, non-Gaussian, and outlier-contaminated measurement conditions.

  • Extended Kalman Filter as the primary model-based estimator.
  • Variational Quantum Regression for learning adaptive state corrections.
  • Innovation-based feature engineering and residual modeling.
  • Testing with simulated nonlinear systems and real GNSS receiver data.
  • Comparison against classical and learning-based filtering baselines.
